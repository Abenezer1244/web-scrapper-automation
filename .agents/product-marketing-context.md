# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-04-15*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** "Set it. Wake up to leads." — Automated motivated seller lead generation from county public records.
**What it does:** BridgeLeads scrapes county recorder websites daily using AI-powered browser automation, enriches each record with property and mailing addresses, and delivers clean CSV lead lists to real estate investors on a schedule.
**Product category:** Real estate lead generation software / PropTech data tool
**Product type:** Multi-tenant SaaS (B2B)
**Business model:** Freemium + tiered subscriptions
- Starter: Free (50 records/mo, 1 county)
- Pro: $49/mo (500 records/mo, 5 counties)
- Business: $149/mo (5,000 records/mo, unlimited counties, API)
- Agency: $499/mo (unlimited records, white-label)

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Solo real estate investors, wholesaling teams, fix-and-flip operations, investor-focused agents, lead generation agencies
**Decision-makers:** The investor themselves (solo) or team leads / acquisition managers
**Primary use case:** Get fresh, enriched motivated seller leads from county public records without manual searching
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Get a list of recent probate filings with property and mailing addresses so I can send yellow letters this week"
- "Get fresh probate and pre-foreclosure leads from 3-5 counties delivered to my inbox every morning"
- "Automate lead generation across all our markets with one tool"
**Use cases:**
- Direct mail campaigns targeting probate heirs, pre-foreclosure owners, tax delinquent properties
- Cold calling campaigns using skip-traced phone numbers
- Building a lead pipeline across multiple counties/states
- White-label lead delivery for agencies serving investor clients

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Real estate investors need fresh motivated seller leads from county public records, but accessing them is painful — 3,100+ counties each with different websites, manual search, no property addresses, hours of work per county per week.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- PropStream/BatchLeads resell bulk data that's 30-90 days stale — by then competitors already contacted the seller
- Manual searching takes 10+ hours/week per county
- List providers charge $200-500/mo for the same stale data
- Goliath Data offers daily freshness but charges $1,499/mo
**What it costs them:** Lost deals (first investor to reach the seller wins), wasted direct mail spend on stale leads, 40+ hours/month of manual work
**Emotional tension:** Fear of missing deals to faster competitors, frustration with repetitive manual work, anxiety about ROI on lead spend

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** PropStream ($99-699/mo) — market leader but data is 30-90 days stale, just acquired BatchLeads creating pricing uncertainty. BatchLeads ($71-449/mo) — now PropStream-owned, doubled prices, deletes data on cancel.
**Secondary:** Goliath Data ($99-1,499/mo) — also scrapes directly but charges 3-10x more for the same freshness advantage. DealMachine ($49-249/mo) — driving-for-dollars focused, not public records.
**Indirect:** Manual county website searching (free but 10+ hrs/week), hiring a VA ($500-1,500/mo), buying one-off lists from list brokers.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- **Daily freshness from the source** — scrapes county portals directly, not resold bulk data
- **AI-powered county onboarding** — paste a URL, Claude navigates the portal automatically. No per-county engineering.
- **Price-to-value ratio** — Pro at $49/mo is half PropStream with fresher data
- **Action caching** — first AI scrape costs ~$0.15, subsequent runs replay cached actions (free)
**How we do it differently:** AI browser automation (Claude) analyzes any county website via screenshots, fills forms, extracts records, and caches the navigation. Competitors either resell aggregated data or require weeks of custom engineering per county.
**Why that's better:** Investors get same-day leads instead of month-old data. Adding new counties takes seconds, not weeks.
**Why customers choose us:** Fresher data at lower cost, with the ability to add any county in the US.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Only covers WA state right now" | We're expanding state by state — WA is live with 22 counties, and our AI can add any US county in 30 seconds. Request your county and we'll add it. |
| "Does it include phone/email?" | Yes — skip tracing is built in. Every lead gets phone, phone type, and email via Tracerfy, delivered within 10-15 minutes of the scrape. |
| "PropStream has more features" | PropStream is an all-in-one tool with month-old data. BridgeLeads is a focused lead source with daily-fresh data. Use us for leads, keep your other tools for workflow. |

**Anti-persona:** Agents who only want MLS data (expired listings, FSBOs). Investors who need a full CRM/dialer/mail platform today. Users who only work in states we haven't expanded to yet.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Data staleness (30-90 day lag), price hikes (BatchLeads doubled prices), data deletion on cancel, PropStream acquisition uncertainty
**Pull:** Daily-fresh data direct from county source, half the price, AI-powered county addition, free tier to try risk-free
**Habit:** Familiar with PropStream UI, existing saved lists and filters, team already trained on current tool
**Anxiety:** "What if BridgeLeads shuts down?" (startup risk), "Will it cover my counties?" (coverage gaps), "No skip tracing yet" (feature gaps)
