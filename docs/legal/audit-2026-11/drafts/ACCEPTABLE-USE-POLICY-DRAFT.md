## 1. Why this policy exists

Lead data names real people, often at a hard moment: a death in the family, a divorce, a foreclosure, an
auction, unpaid property taxes. You may use BridgeLeads only in ways that are lawful and that treat those
people fairly. This policy is part of the Terms of Service. Breaking it is a material breach.

## 2. You are responsible for your outreach

You are responsible for every call, text, email, letter and visit you make using lead data, and for following
every law that applies to it, including (as examples only):

- telemarketing, texting and Do-Not-Call laws, federal and state, including calling-time and consent rules;
- commercial email law (for example CAN-SPAM);
- foreclosure-rescue, distressed-property and equity-purchaser laws, including any required notices, forms and
  cancellation periods;
- elder and vulnerable-adult financial-exploitation laws;
- fair housing and anti-discrimination laws;
- state privacy laws that apply to your own business.

## 3. Phone numbers and Do-Not-Call

- **BridgeLeads does not screen phone numbers against any Do-Not-Call registry, and leads carry no Do-Not-Call
  indicator.** A blank or "unknown" status does not mean a number may be called.
- You must screen every number yourself before you call or text it, and keep any consent the law requires.
- Phone numbers and emails come from a third-party provider and can be wrong or belong to someone else.

## 4. Prohibited uses

You must not:

1. Use the service or any lead data as a consumer report, or to decide anyone's eligibility for credit,
   insurance, employment, housing or tenancy, or any other purpose covered by the Fair Credit Reporting Act.
   BridgeLeads is not a consumer reporting agency. `[COUNSEL: question C-7]`
2. Harass, threaten, stalk, deceive or pressure anyone, or use misleading scripts, false urgency or false
   claims of affiliation with a lender, court, county or government.
3. Discriminate against anyone on a protected basis, or target people for a protected characteristic.
4. Contact a person in a way the law forbids, for example a number on a Do-Not-Call list without a lawful
   basis, or outside permitted hours.
5. Resell, publish, give away or share lead data or contact data with anyone outside your business, except as
   the Terms expressly allow. `[COUNSEL: affiliates, team members, buyers' lists]`
6. Use lead data to build or improve a competing data product.
7. Scrape, crawl or bulk-download the BridgeLeads site or app, or access it other than through the app or our
   documented API.
8. Get around usage limits, rate limits, plan limits or security controls, or share credentials or API keys
   outside your business.
9. Probe, scan or test the service's security without our written permission. Report security problems to
   security@bridgeleads.io instead.
10. Use the service to send malware, spam or unlawful content through a connected integration.
11. Upload personal information you have no right to share with us.

## 5. Privacy requests

When a person named in our records opts out or asks for deletion, we stop delivering records that name them,
including in your future deliveries and integrations. You must not try to identify, re-collect or contact a
person because they made such a request.
