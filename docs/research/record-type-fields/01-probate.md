Heirs cannot bind the estate. This makes the PR name and mailing address the single most important fields. ([RealEstateSkills wholesaling guide](https://www.realestateskills.com/blog/probate-wholesaling), [SmartSkip](https://smartskip.io/resources/blog/finding-executor-estate-probate))\n\n### Contact Priority Order\n\n**1. Personal Representative — Primary contact, always first.**\n- Mail to PR mailing address (not the property address — the PR often lives elsewhere).\n- Call PR phone numbers (up to 5 skip-traced numbers).\n- Email PR if address available.\n- If PR mailing address = subject property address, do NOT skip — the PR may live there or be managing the property on-site.\n- Key data needed: PR full name, PR mailing address, PR phone(s), PR email.\n\n**2. Estate Attorney — Secondary / relationship-building track.**\n- Many PRs forward all investor inquiries to their attorney first.\n- Building relationships with probate attorneys generates deal flow upstream of the list — attorneys refer motivated clients.\n- Separate outreach track (B2B mail/email to attorney office).\n- Key data needed: Attorney name, attorney firm address, attorney phone.\n- ([AllTheLeads email marketing guide](https://www.alltheleads.com/find-probate-homes-real-estate-leads-email-marketing/))\n\n**3. Heirs — Tertiary / influence track, not primary.**\n- Heirs cannot sign contracts but they CAN influence the PR's decision.\n- Useful when the PR is unresponsive or when multiple heirs are in conflict (early contact with the most motivated heir can create internal pressure).\n- Out-of-state heirs are the highest-value targets here.\n- Key data needed: Heir names, heir addresses, heir relationship to decedent (spouse &lt; adult child, sibling &lt; adult child for motivation).\n- ([ProbateData motivation guide](https://www.probatedata.com/blog/what-makes-probate-sellers-motivated-complete-guide))\n\n### Outreach Channels (in order of effectiveness for probate)\n1. Direct mail to PR mailing address — industry consensus first touch; least intrusive in an emotional situation.\n2. Phone call to PR — 24-hour response window boosts conversion 650000000000000000er ProbateData benchmarks.\n3. Email to PR / attorney — second touch; works well when combined with mail.\n4. In-person court attendance — advanced strategy; investors attend probate hearings to meet PRs and attorneys live.\n\n---\n\n## Part 4 — Timing: Which Date Matters and When to Act\n\n### The Four Relevant Dates\n\n| Date | What It Signals | Investor Use |\n|---|---|---|\n| **Date of Death** | Estate clock starts; family is in acute grief | Too early to contact (days to ~3 weeks); useful for pre-probate strategies via obituary monitoring |\n| **Probate Filing Date** | Case is open; PR named; legal process has started | The primary freshness field; most providers surface this. Investors calculate \"months elapsed since filing.\" |\n| **Letters Testamentary / Administration Issued Date** | PR has legal authority to act; can sign a contract | Gate check before making an offer; without letters, the PR technically cannot execute |\n| **Case Close / Final Distribution Date** | Estate is settled; property already transferred or sold | Too late; lead is dead |\n\n### The Optimal Outreach Window\n- **Weeks 1–4 post-filing:** Too sensitive. Initial grief. Do not cold call.\n- **Months 1–3 post-filing:** Begin first-touch direct mail (soft, empathetic tone). No aggressive follow-up.\n- **Months 4–6 post-filing:** Prime window. Bills have accumulated (average $1,963/month in carrying costs per ProbateData). PR is actively looking for solutions. Conversion rates peak here.\n- **Months 7–12:** Urgency intensifies near court deadlines. Good window but some leads have already been worked by competitors.\n- **12+ months:** Flag as potentially complicated (litigation, disputes, title issues). Nurture only.\n\n([ProbateData qualification guide](https://www.probatedata.com/blog/what-makes-a-probate-lead-qualified-complete-guide-2026), [ProbateData motivation guide](https://www.probatedata.com/blog/what-makes-probate-sellers-motivated-complete-guide), [PropertyRadar blog](https://www.propertyradar.com/blog/probate-public-records-leads))\n\n### Freshness Requirement\nCourt-sourced lists that are more than 4–6 weeks old at time of delivery are already degraded.
